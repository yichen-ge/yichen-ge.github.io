About Me

I am a master’s student in Artificial Intelligence at China University of Petroleum (Beijing), advised by Assoc. Prof. Liping Zhu. I also collaborate closely with Assoc. Prof. Chengyang Li.

My current research focuses on embodied intelligence, particularly its data challenges. I am interested in how to scale up real-world data and cost-effectively build training datasets and benchmarks for embodied intelligence. My previous research focused on generative models and human motion generation, particularly autoregressive generation and text-to-motion generation.

I am actively seeking Ph.D. opportunities starting in 2027.
